Create faculty load

Select a faculty, then parse a load command. Load = (lecture + lab hours) ÷ 3 per offering (scheduled contact hours ÷ 3). Suggested baseline: Regular = 8, Part-time = 3 (you can assign more). Use 8 loads for a total teaching load of 8 (8.33 or 8.67 is fine; never 9+ unless you add extra load separately). Use 8 load IT 322 for a specific subject. Use a smaller number (e.g. 2 loads) only when adding extra load to someone who is already full.

Time filter (optional): 3 loads 5pm up — offerings at 5:00 PM or later; 3 loads 7 am to 9 am — offerings within 7:00–9:00 AM; 3 loads CICT-ATT 5pm up — subject + time filter.
